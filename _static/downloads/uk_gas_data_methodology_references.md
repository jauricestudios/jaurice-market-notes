# UK Gas Price Pressure and Exposure Model — Data & Methodology References

**National Gas Transmission.** (2026). *Gas Data Portal: UK gas operational data, storage stock levels, LNG terminal flows and market-balancing indicators*. National Gas Transmission. Available at: https://data.nationalgas.com/  
Used for: UK system gas indicators, Rough storage stock levels, long-range storage data and LNG terminal outflow context.

**National Gas Transmission.** (2026). *System Average Price data*. National Gas Transmission Data Portal. Available at: https://data.nationalgas.com/  
Used for: UK gas price proxy and commercial buyer exposure calculations.
