# Mining Investment Model

_Last updated: 17 June 2026_

This page will apply pricing, forecasting and NPV modelling to a mining-style investment case.

## Example project

A copper or lithium project where revenue depends on price assumptions, production volumes, operating costs, capex and discount rate.

## Outputs

- commodity price chart
- production assumption table
- revenue forecast
- operating cost forecast
- NPV calculation
- price sensitivity table
- downside scenario
- investment interpretation
