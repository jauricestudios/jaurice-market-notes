# Energy Market Research Portfolio

_Last updated: 16 June 2026_

A chart-based research site tracking UK gas, LNG, GB power and commodity risk using public data, Python and Excel.

The main focus is UK gas market tightness: storage, LNG send-out, interconnector flows, NBP vs TTF spreads, weather demand and GB power flexibility.
