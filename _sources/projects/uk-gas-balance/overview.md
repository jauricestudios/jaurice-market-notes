# UK Gas Balance Study

_Last updated: 16 June 2026_


This is the main project in the portfolio.

The study asks whether the UK gas market is becoming tighter or looser by combining supply, demand, storage, LNG send-out, interconnector flows, NBP versus TTF spreads and forward curve interpretation.

## Core question

Is UK gas market tightness being driven by domestic demand, European market conditions, LNG availability, storage levels or infrastructure flows?

## Main outputs

| Output | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Gas balance table | Summarises supply, demand, storage and flows |
| NBP versus TTF spread chart | Shows UK price pressure relative to Europe |
| Storage and LNG tracker | Shows whether flexible supply is supporting the market |
| Interconnector flow table | Shows whether the UK is importing from or exporting to Europe |
| Market note | Explains the current signal in plain English |
| Data source table | Makes the project reproducible |

## Why this matters

Gas markets are not only about price charts. Physical flows matter. Storage matters. Weather matters. LNG cargoes matter. Interconnectors matter.

The aim of this project is to connect those pieces into one clear market view.
