# Energy Market Strategy & Risk Portfolio

_Last updated: 16 June 2026_

This portfolio studies UK and European gas, LNG and wholesale power markets through market research, Python, Excel, GIS and mathematical modelling. The focus is on energy security, forward curves, storage value, system flexibility and market risk.

The central idea is that the energy transition does not remove energy risk. It changes where the bottlenecks appear. As renewables grow, markets become more exposed to storage, grids, LNG competition, critical minerals, flexible generation and physical bottlenecks affecting prices.

## What This Portfolio Shows

- **Physical market reasoning:** storage, flows, weather, infrastructure and policy.
- **Wholesale market analysis:** balances, forward curves, spreads, residual demand and spark spreads.
- **Quantitative modelling:** Python, Excel, Monte Carlo, scenarios and basic risk models.
- **Strategic research:** European energy security, Saudi Vision 2030, critical minerals and geopolitics.
- **Analyst writing:** turning charts, sources and assumptions into market interpretation.

## Research Questions Behind the Work

- How do storage, LNG flows and interconnectors affect UK/EU energy security?
- What do forward curves reveal about seasonal scarcity and storage value?
- When do renewables increase the value of flexibility, storage and gas-fired generation?
- How can Monte Carlo and stress scenarios value physical energy optionality?
- How do Saudi Vision 2030, Europe’s energy independence and critical minerals reshape energy-market structure?
