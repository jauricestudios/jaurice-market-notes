# Energy Market Research Portfolio

_Last updated: 16 June 2026_

This site is a working portfolio of energy market monitors focused on UK gas, LNG, GB power and commodity risk.

The aim is to turn public market data into clear charts, balance tables and short market interpretations.

## Build standard

Every main page should contain at least one of the following:

- chart
- table
- calculation
- source list
- short interpretation

Pages that do not yet have outputs are kept out of the main navigation.
