<h1>Commodity &amp; Energy Transition Research</h1>

<p>
A research portfolio focused on energy markets, commodity supply chains and infrastructure risk,
with an emphasis on UK and European gas, LNG, power markets and transition-critical minerals.
</p>

<p>
The aim is to combine <strong>Python</strong>, <strong>Excel</strong>, <strong>GIS</strong> and market research to build
static dashboard prototypes, indicator frameworks and analyst-style notes.
</p>

<p>
This site is designed to show how physical market constraints such as storage, flows, weather,
infrastructure, policy and geopolitics can become financial and commercial risk signals.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Tools:</strong> Python · Excel · GIS · Market Research · Dashboard Prototyping · Energy Market Analysis
</p>
