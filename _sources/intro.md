<h1>Commodity &amp; Energy Transition Research</h1>

<p>
This site is a collection of research notes, dashboards and modelling projects on energy markets,
commodities, infrastructure risk and applied market analytics.
</p>

<p>
The aim is to use <strong>Python</strong>, <strong>Excel</strong>, <strong>GIS</strong> and market reasoning to understand how
physical energy systems become financial and commercial risks.
</p>

<p>
My current focus is UK and European gas and power markets, LNG, storage, critical minerals,
geopolitics and the wider energy transition.
</p>
