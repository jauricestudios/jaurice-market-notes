# Pricing, Forecasting & Commercial Analytics

_Last updated: 17 June 2026_

This site is a working portfolio of Excel, SQL and Python projects for pricing, forecasting, risk and commercial decision-making.

The work is not about pretending to find the true price of a commodity. Market prices already exist. The aim is to use data to analyse prices, spreads, costs, forecasts, margins, project value, logistics and risk.

## What This Portfolio Is For

The aim is to build projects that are useful for pricing analyst, commercial analyst, insurance pricing, energy analyst, risk analyst, forecasting, logistics and supply chain roles.

The work is based around six practical questions:

1. What is the price, cost, margin or risk signal?
2. What data explains it?
3. What happens under a different scenario?
4. How reliable is the forecast or model?
5. What are the limitations?
6. What is the commercial implication?

## Skills Shown

- **Excel:** XLOOKUP, PivotTables, Power Query, LET, LAMBDA, scenario tables, dashboards and basic VBA.
- **SQL:** joins, filtering, grouped summaries, date handling and clean data extraction.
- **Python:** pandas, matplotlib, statsmodels, time-series analysis, model validation and chart generation.
- **Pricing analytics:** rating factors, margin checks, spread analysis, delivered cost and scenario pricing.
- **Insurance pricing:** claims frequency, claims severity, exposure, loss ratios, GLM-style pricing and model validation.
- **Risk:** price shocks, rolling volatility, exposure tables, stress testing and forecast error.
