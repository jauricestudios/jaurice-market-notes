<h1>Physical Commodity &amp; Energy Market Research</h1>

<p>
This is my research portfolio for physical commodity and energy markets, with an emphasis on UK/EU gas,
LNG, wholesale power, forward curves, storage value, energy strategy and market risk.
</p>

<p>
The portfolio is built around a simple idea: the energy transition shifts risk toward
<strong>security of supply</strong>, <strong>system flexibility</strong>, <strong>storage</strong>,
<strong>grids</strong>, <strong>LNG competition</strong>, <strong>critical minerals</strong>
and the pricing of physical constraints.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Current outputs:</strong> UK gas balance framework · NBP/TTF spread analysis · forward-curve interpretation ·
Excel dashboard prototype · market notes · modelling prototypes.
</p>

</p>
