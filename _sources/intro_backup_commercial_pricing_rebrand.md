_Last updated: 17 June 2026_

This site is a portfolio of commodity and energy analytics projects focused on UK gas, LNG, GB power and market risk.

The aim is to turn public market data into forecasting models, pricing analysis, Excel commercial tools, Python notebooks and clear market interpretation.

## Project Direction

The portfolio is moving from simple monitoring toward forecasting, pricing and commercial analytics.

The core workflow is:

1. collect market data
2. clean and structure it
3. visualise time series
4. build Excel and Python models
5. test forecasts
6. interpret commercial implications
7. document sources and limitations

## Skills Demonstrated

- **Excel:** XLOOKUP, PivotTables, Power Query, LET, LAMBDA, scenario tables, dashboard design and basic VBA.
- **Python:** pandas, matplotlib, statsmodels, time-series cleaning, forecasting and model validation.
- **Commercial analytics:** spreads, storage value, gas balance, power burn, volatility, scenario analysis and market interpretation.
- **Data discipline:** source tables, assumptions, reproducibility and limitations.
